# Code of Conduct

We want ChroNicos to be a welcoming, constructive space for everyone who uses
or contributes to the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Focus feedback on the work, explain disagreements constructively, and accept
  good-faith corrections.
- Respect people's privacy and do not publish personal information without
  permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of those involved, and
may edit or remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
